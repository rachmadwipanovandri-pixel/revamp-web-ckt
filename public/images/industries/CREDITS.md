# Industry photography

Every file in this folder is a real `<img>` source: the homepage industry
directory (`src/components/sections/home/industry-carousel-panels.tsx`) uses the
eight squares below as row thumbnails and open-row portraits, and the `/industri`
hub cards (`src/components/sections/landing/entry-card.tsx`) use all 29 files as
16:10 covers.

## Homepage squares (supplied by the Cekat team)

Sources were supplied by the Cekat team through Google Drive. Use them only
under the usage rights provided by the team; this file makes no external stock
licence claim.

Each supplied image was reframed into a square, resized to 700×700, and encoded
as WebP at q72. When replacing an image, prepare a square source again so the
subject remains intact in both states.

**The square is load-bearing, not a whim.** One file has to survive two very
different frames: the open portrait is landscape (around 483×336) and a closed
row thumbnail is small (48×36). A square preserves the composition's centre in
both states, whereas a portrait or landscape source loses too much of its subject
when `object-fit: cover` crops it. Keep the primary subject or product near the
middle when preparing a replacement.
